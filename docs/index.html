How to hide YouTube Shorts

Ways to reduce Shorts distractions

Choose an approach for the device you use. Hiding a shelf, hiding web page elements and limiting app time have different effects. Read the desktop and mobile guide.

Method 1 · Built into YouTube

"Not interested" & the X button

Where available, use YouTube's dismissal or "Not interested" controls to adjust recommendations. Available controls vary by device and account; dismissing one shelf does not disable all Shorts entry points.

Recommendation controls · limited scope

Method 2 · On your phone

App timers & Digital Wellbeing

Browser extensions do not run inside the YouTube iPhone or Android app. Device app timers can limit total YouTube app time; they do not selectively remove Shorts. Browser-based alternatives depend on the browser and extension.

Limits time · doesn't remove Shorts

Method 3 · Browser extension

Focuvia, on desktop

The Focuvia development build hides supported Shorts elements on desktop YouTube, with separate controls for recommendations, comments and watch limits. Public store links are not available yet. The development packages target Chrome/Edge 120+ and Firefox 142+.

What Focuvia does

A quieter YouTube watch page

Watch the video you came for, without a recommendation sidebar or comments pulling you away. Bring back video details when you need them, keep Shorts out of the way, and choose how much time to spend watching.

Video details, on hover

The top bar, title, channel and description stay hidden until you need them. Hover to bring them back without moving the player. The top bar also appears when its controls receive keyboard focus.

Daily watch limit

Choose your daily watch time — start with 30 minutes. When time’s up, the video pauses. Add 10 minutes or stop for the day. Your progress carries across tabs and refreshes.

Hide YouTube Shorts

Hide YouTube Shorts shelves, cards and navigation links on supported desktop pages. Regular video search results stay available; coverage depends on YouTube’s layout.

Hide YouTube recommendations

Open a video without a sidebar of suggested next watches. Optionally hide the homepage feed, too: search for what you need, watch it, close the tab. These are separate controls. Autoplay and suggestions inside the player’s end screen are not changed.

Hide comments

Stay with the video instead of drifting into the discussion below it. Comments are hidden by default; turn the setting off whenever you want to read or join the conversation.

Estimated dislike counts

See estimated dislike counts from Return YouTube Dislike below the player. These are third-party estimates, not official YouTube counts.

Privacy & control

Hiding runs locally, with no Focuvia account or analytics. Settings use browser sync; watch totals stay on this device. Estimated dislike counts send the current video ID to a third-party API and are enabled by default. Read the privacy details.

Edge 120+

Download for Edge
  1. Download and unzip the file.

  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the extracted folder.

Firefox 142+

Download for Firefox
  1. Download and unzip the file.
  2. Open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox.
  3. Click Load Temporary Add-on and select manifest.json in the extracted folder.

Firefox removes this unsigned preview when it restarts. Load it again to keep using it.

After installing, refresh your YouTube tabs. To update, extract the new download, reload the extension, then refresh YouTube.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can Focuvia permanently block YouTube Shorts?+

Focuvia hides recognized Shorts elements while enabled on supported desktop YouTube pages. It does not block network access to Shorts URLs or prevent you from disabling the extension. YouTube layout changes can affect coverage and may require an extension update.

What does Focuvia hide on the YouTube watch page?+

Focuvia hides the recommendation sidebar and comments. The top bar, title, channel and description stay hidden until you hover over their areas. Playback controls remain available. Each hiding option can be changed in the extension popup.

Can I set a daily YouTube time limit?+

Yes. Choose a daily watch limit — 30 minutes by default. Visible, playing videos count toward it. When time’s up, playback pauses. Add 10 minutes or stop for the day; progress carries across tabs and refreshes on this device.

Can I install Focuvia on Chrome or Firefox now?+

Yes. Use the browser download links and installation steps above for Chrome/Edge 120+ or Firefox 142+. Install Chrome and Edge using Load unpacked. Firefox uses Load Temporary Add-on; the unsigned preview is removed when Firefox restarts. Browser-store installation is not available yet.

Can I use Focuvia on iPhone or Android?+

The current build targets the desktop www.youtube.com website, not m.youtube.com or the YouTube iPhone and Android apps. Some other extensions support specific mobile browsers; check each extension’s listing. Device app timers limit app time rather than selectively hiding Shorts.

Is Focuvia free?+

The current development build is free, with no subscription, Focuvia account or ads. Public store installation is not available yet.

What data does Focuvia store or send?+

Settings use browser sync storage and may sync through your browser account. Viewing totals, extra time and stopped state use local storage on this device. Estimated dislike counts are enabled by default and send the current video ID to Return YouTube Dislike, whose server also receives connection information such as your IP address. Turning that feature off cancels the active request and stops further dislike requests. Focuvia has no analytics or backend.

How is Focuvia different from UnTrap?+

Focuvia’s development build focuses on hiding distractions, a daily watch budget and estimated dislike counts; it does not include AI summaries. UnTrap lists hiding controls, summaries, schedules and other tools. Its official site and Firefox listing describe different plan boundaries, so check the linked sources for your platform. Focuvia is not publicly available in stores yet.

Will Focuvia hide videos I search for?+

Regular video search results remain available. Hide Shorts targets recognized Shorts cards and shelves in search results. Coverage depends on YouTube’s layout and may require updates. You can turn Hide Shorts off. Hiding elements does not prevent opening a Shorts URL directly.
